What is tracked in Experience

Ellucian Experience uses non-essential cookies and third-party analytics tools to help Ellucian understand how users interact with our product. This information helps us improve your experience by refining features and optimizing the functionality of the platform for your daily use.

Cookies

Cookies make your experience smoother and help us understand what you enjoy most. Here’s how they work:
  1. When you visit a site, the site places a cookie on your device. This cookie is like a digital note that can store information, such as what pages you visit or how long you stay.
  2. As you interact with the site, the cookie collects data (such as clicks, time spent, and your device type).
  3. The cookie sends this data back to the site’s analytics software. This software gathers and organizes data from many users to show patterns, such as which pages are popular or where people tend to leave.
  4. The site uses these patterns to improve features, fix issues, or make pages more useful based on what people are actually doing on the site.

What We Measure

We track general interactions with our platform, such as:
  • Which pages you visit most often (for example, dashboard or profile page).
  • How you engage with key features, such as buttons or menus.
  • Your overall usage patterns, such as time spent on certain sections.
  • Navigational paths through the platform to identify common workflows.
  • Engagement with in-app guides, messages, and prompts.
  • Which device you are using to access the portal, along with the browser type, browser version and operating system.

Why We Collect This Data

We collect this information to:
  • Understand which features are most valuable to users.
  • Identify areas for improvement and make the platform more user-friendly.
  • Track general trends in how users move through the platform, which helps us make data-driven decisions for new updates.

How We Use This Data

To answer questions like “How are students using this feature?”, the analytics tools use data from cookies you allowed. This data shows general behavior by role (like student or faculty) within a feature, all under a secure ID—no names or personal details are attached.

The goal is to understand how groups, such as students across different universities, interact with the platform. By working with large data sets, patterns emerge, making it easier to improve the experience for everyone without focusing on any individual’s data.

Your Control

You have full control over whether non-essential cookies are used or not. Essential cookies are required for the platform to work properly, but you can opt in or out of additional analytics cookies at any time using the Share Usage Data setting in the user profile. If you opt out, only essential cookies will be used.

For more detailed information, please review the full Ellucian Privacy Notice.
