Higher Education Authority (HEA) in Banner

The HEA is the statutory planning and policy development body for higher education and research in Ireland and has wide advisory powers throughout the whole of the third-level education sector. It is also the funding authority for the universities, Institutes of Technology and a number of designated higher education institutions.

To facilitate their work, the HEA require Universities, Information Technologies and the designated Higher Education institutions to make returns, twice yearly, the provisional return in November and the final return in March. The returns include Program, Course, and Student details. Banner HEA.

System requirements

The Banner Student release supports the baseline and localized versions of the application.

Refer to the Banner Administrative Pages Installation Guide and the Banner Student Installation Reference for the minimum system requirements.

Note: Institutions in Ireland who want to leverage the HEA functionality delivered in the December 2019 release of Banner Student must have installed Banner General 9.3.13 or the above version in their environments.

Types of returns

Types of reports that the institutions can return to HEA.

Program return

A program is the general or un-denominated area of study that a course belongs to, for example, Bachelor of Engineering, Bachelor of Arts, or Diploma in Business Studies.

If the academic structure in your institution is highly denominated, the entries for program and course will most likely be the same. An example of the latter is where a student has enrolled on a program, Bachelor of Business Studies with French or a National Diploma in Computing and there are no streams or options available below the level of program. The program file contains descriptors such as program name, faculty, program type (that is, Undergraduate Degree).

Course return

A course is a denominated area of study within a program, for instance, Bachelor of Engineering in Electronic and Electrical Engineering.

The student has enrolled on the program Bachelor of Engineering but is pursuing a more specific area of study. The course file contains descriptors such as course name, course code, and awarding body.

Survey return

The Survey Return file contains the individual student or graduate records.

A broad range of data is collected; student details such as age, gender, country of origin; course details such as course being pursued, ISCED code of this course, mode of study (full-time/part-time) and subject data (where further breakdown is required, for example, BA Arts, B Sc Science).

Access Survey return

This file contains student bio-demo specific information, such as physical disability, learning disability, ethnicity, psychological, emotional, or mental health, parental status and so on. This information is optional for the students to return to HEA.

Storing data for HEA returns in Banner

Banner provides a built-in functionality that allow institutions to store data and generate XML files for HEA returns. This section discusses the step-by-step procedures that the institutions must follow to store the HEA specific data.

Create program and course information

Use the following steps to add HEA specific program and course information.

About this task

HEA programme is the same as Banner SMAPRLE program and HEA course is same as the Banner STVMAJR major.

Procedure

  1. Access the HEA Regulatory Programme and Course Details (SOAPRCD) page.
  2. In the PROGRAMME DETAILS section, enter the programme related information.
  3. In the FACULTY DETAILS section, enter the faculty information related to the programme.
  4. In the COURSE DETAILS section, enter the course information related to the programme for all the relevant academic years.
  5. Save the record.
    Refer to the Banner_Student_HEA_Seed_Data_August_2020 excel spreadsheet that is available as an attachment along with the Banner Student Release Notes 8.20 and 9.3.20 version.

Create student information

Use the following steps to create student information.

Procedure

  1. Access the General Person Identification (SPAIDEN) page.
  2. Enter and store person information in the Current Identification window and Address window.
  3. Access the General Person (SPAPERS) page.
  4. Enter and store biographical information in the Biographical window.
  5. Access the International Information (GOAINTL) page.
  6. Enter and store nation information in the Nation of Citizen field of the Nationality window.
  7. Access the Person Social Economic Identity Details (GOAPSID) page.
  8. Enter and store the other biographical and demo-graphical information of the student under the HEA_RETURN report code.
    The HEA_RETURN report code is used for Survey and Access Survey report generation. Hence, this report code and its associated configuration must not be altered.
  9. Access the Access the High School (SOAHSCH) page.
  10. Enter and store student’s previous high school information in the High School Details window.
  11. Access the Prior College (SOAPCOL) page.
  12. Enter and store the student’s previous college information in the Prior College and Degree window.

Create student course information

Use the following steps to create student course information.

About this task

The major code entered in the Field of Study field in the Curricula window of the SGASTDN page is considered as the course code attached to a specific program of the student. The course code (Field of Study) must be unique across all the programs of a student. The combination of the program and course of the student must be available in the SOAPRCD page. The course information in this page should be available for the academic year that needs to be returned for the student.

Procedure

  1. Access the HEA Student Course and Funding Details (SOASCFD) page.
  2. In the Key block, enter the student details for which you want to record the course and funding details.
  3. Go to the next block.
  4. In the Base section, enter the year details of the course in the YEAR ON COURSE DETAILS section.
  5. In the STUDENT DETAILS – Student Information section, enter the student details.
  6. In the COURSE INFORMATION section enter the student course information.
  7. In the PREVIOUS ACADEMIC HISTORY section, enter the student's previous academic history details.
  8. In the FEES AND FUNDING INFORMATION section, enter the student's fees and funding details.
  9. Save the record.

Map Banner codes to HEA codes

Use the following steps to map Banner codes to the HEA codes.

Procedure

  1. Access the Integration Configuration Settings (GORICCR) page.
  2. In the Process field select the HEA process code.
  3. Click the Setting LOV and select the setting code from the Integration Configuration window.
  4. For the HEA_ADDRESS_TYPE setting, update the Value field with relevant address code from the STVATYP page. Based on this mapping, the address details are fetched for the students from the SPRADDR during the report generation process.
  5. For the HEA_COUNTY_CODE setting, update the Value field with the county code from the STVCNTY page that is equivalent to the HEA code provided in the Translation field.
  6. For the HEA_GENDER setting, update the Value field with the gender code from the GTVGNDR page that is equivalent to the HEA code provided in the Translation field.
  7. For the HEA_STUDENT_CODE setting, update the Value field with the student type code from the STVSTYP page that is equivalent to the HEA code provided in the Translation field.
  8. Save the record.

Create student population for reporting

Use the following steps to create student population for reporting. This is needed only for the HEA Survey and HEA Access Survey returns.

Procedure

  1. Access the Population Selection Extract Data (GLAEXTR) page.
  2. Enter STUDENT in the Application field.
  3. Enter the selection ID in the Selection ID field that you have created in the Population Selection Definition Rules page (GLRSLCT) page.
  4. Go to the next block.
  5. In the POPULATION SELECTION EXTRACT DATA section, list and store the student IDs.
    To know more about how to build a population selection and the procedures, refer to the Population Selection section in the Banner General User Guide.

Generate HEA returns in Banner

Use the following step-by-step procedure to generate the HEA reports.

Procedure

  1. Access the Process Submission Control (GJAPCTL) page.
  2. Enter SORHEAR in the Process field.
  3. Go to the next block.
  4. In the Printer Control block, enter DATABASE in the Printer field.
  5. In the Parameter Values block, enter the values for the following parameters:
    1. Program Return: Enter Y for generating the return
    2. Course Return: Enter Y for generating the return
    3. Access Survey Return: Enter Y for generating the return
    4. Survey Return: Enter Y for generating the return

      The parameter values are always set to N by default. Institutions can choose to generate either one return, a combination of the returns or all the returns, at one time.

    5. Return Year: Enter the year for which you want the returns. For example, for the academic year 2018/2019, enter the return year as 2019.
    6. Institute Code: Enter the code that is specific to your institution

      Refer to HEA Institute Codes section to see the list of available institute codes provided by HEA. Institutions must enter the relevant codes from this list.

    7. Application Code: Enter the application code for the list of students in the Population Selection Extract Data (GLAEXTR) page
    8. Selection Code: Enter the selection code for the list of students in the GLAEXTR page
    9. Creator ID: Enter the creator ID for the list of students in the GLAEXTR page
    10. User ID: Enter the user ID for the list of students in the GLAEXTR page
    11. Return Type: Click the Values column and select the Final or Provisional value from the Parameter Values window.
      The combination of application code, selection code, creator ID, and user ID defines the student population selection (in the GLAEXTR page) for which the Survey or Access Survey report is to be generated. These four parameters are only needed while generating the Survey or Access Survey returns.
  6. Navigate to the Submission section and click Save.
  7. Click the RELATED option from the page header and select Review Output (GJIREVO) option from the menu.
  8. In the GJIREVO page, select the file name of the report that you want to view.
    The system also generates a log file that the institutions can select from the File Name field LOV. This log file lists the students from the student population selection who are excluded from the generated report due to non-maintenance of appropriate data.
  9. In the SAVED OUTPUT REVIEW section, you can see the HEA report generated.
  10. Click the TOOLS option from the page header and select the Show Document (Save and Print File) option from the menu to download the XML report to your system’s Downloads folder.

Academic year and return year

An academic year duration for HEA is in the YYYY/YYYY format. For example, for a HEA academic year starting in September 2018 and ending in August 2019, the format is 2018/2019.

An academic year in Banner, that is created and stored in the STVACYR page, is equivalent to starting year of the HEA academic year (2018 in Banner). A return year is considered as ending year of the HEA academic year (2019 in Banner).

Program return XML

This file consists of information related to the programs offered by the institution, stored in the SOAPRCD page. If a program is marked as “Exclude from reporting”, in the SOAPRCD page, then the program will not get picked for reporting.

Note: For the report to fetch a program, the program details must be stored in the Program Details block in the SOAPRCD page.

Refer to the Programme Return tab of the Banner_Student_HEA_Returns_Fields_Mapping_December_2019.xls spreadsheet to know more about the data storage of programme return fields in Banner.

Course return XML

This file consists of information related to the courses offered by the institution, stored in the SOAPRCD page. If a program is marked as “Exclude from reporting” in the SOAPRCD page, then all the courses associated with the program will not get picked for reporting.

Note: For the report to fetch a course, the course and program details must be stored in the Course Details and Program Details block in the SOAPRCD page. Also, ensure that the course is stored for the academic year which must be fetched in a return year.

Refer to the Course Return tab of the Banner_Student_HEA_Returns_Fields_Mapping_August_2020.xls spreadsheet to know more about the data storage of course return fields in Banner.

Survey return XML

This file consists of student and their course related information. The return year entered in the input parameter identifies the courses that the students have studied/studying for that academic year.

Institutions must store the following information to generate the survey report.
  • Student’s Date of Birth / Gender in the SPAPERS page or in the Biographical window of the SPAIDEN page.
  • Student details in the GOAPSID page against the HEA_REPORT code.
  • Details of the Program and Course that the students are enrolled into to be stored in the SOAPRCD page. The course details must be stored for every academic year that needs to be returned.
  • For a Return Year, store the corresponding Banner Academic Year with the Student’s Year on Course on Year on Course Details block in the SOASCFD page.

    For example: In HEA Academic Year 2019/2020 (Banner Academic Year 2019), Sophia is in the second year of Bachelor of Engineering with a major in Information Technology. For the Survey Report to fetch Sophia’s details in the Return Year 2020, the institute must maintain Sophia’s record of Year on Course “2” with Academic Year “2019” in the SOASFDT page.

Refer to the Survey Return tab of the Banner_Student_HEA_Returns_Fields_Mapping_August_2020.xls spreadsheet to know more about the data storage of survey return fields in Banner.
Note: If your institution is using the Banner CAO solution, then ensure that there is an entry present in the GURLCIN table. This entry is specifically used to fetch LC Exams information. If no entry found for the Banner CAO in this table, execute the following query:

insert into GURLCIN (GURLCIN_LCIN_CODE,GURLCIN_ACTIVITY_DATE,GURLCIN_USER_ID) values ('CAO',sysdate,user);

Access survey return XML

This file consists of student’s bio-demo information fetched from the GOAPSID page along with the course code that the student is studying/studied during the academic year of the return year.

Refer to the Access Survey Return tab of the Banner_Student_HEA_Returns_Fields_Mapping_August_2020.xls spreadsheet (available as an attachment), to know more about the data storage of access survey return fields in Banner.

HEA Graduate Outcomes

The annual graduate outcomes survey that happens every academic year is collected in respect of graduates of HEIs approximately nine months after their completion of study.

The data submitted in the record is obtained through a survey instrument, centrally defined by the HEA and locally managed by HEIs.

The annual graduate outcomes survey has one census date, 31 March of each year. The date chosen aims to provide a picture of graduate activity approximately nine months after completion of the graduate’s programme of study. Therefore, completed questionnaires should be stored safely until the data needs to be returned to the HEA. There is a contact period for each survey which sets out when HEIs should contact graduates. Each HEI should identify the most appropriate time period for contacting graduates.

The graduates takes the survey by answering a number of questions by way of a questionnaire.  The survey is first be sent to all relevant graduates electronically and graduates can log into the system using their login details and fill in the survey.

The HEI follows up with the graduates who have not responded after a period of time. The results of the survey is captured in a file that institutions can download by the HEI.

Results of the answers are built into the Banner Student tables specific to HEA Graduate Outcomes, and from those tables, a process in Banner extracts the survey results into XML for sending to HEA.

Refer to the HEA Graduate Outcome Survey (SOAHEGO) page in Banner Student Reference content to know more information about the survey questionnaire and answers.

Refer to the HEA Graduate Outcome Ireland (SORHEGO) process in Banner Student Reports and Process content to know information about the collection of survey responses from the HEA graduates for a specific return year and institution type.
