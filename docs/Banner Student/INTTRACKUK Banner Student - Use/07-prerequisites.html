Prerequisites

The minimum required releases for INTTRACKUK with Attendance Tracking 8.x and 9x.

Prerequisites for Banner INTTRACKUK 8.3.8

The minimum required releases for Banner INTTRACKUK 8.3.8 are as follows:
  • Banner INTTRACKUK 8.3.7
  • Banner Student 8.22.1
  • Banner UCAS 8.15.1
  • Banner HESA 8.4
  • Banner General 8.13
  • Banner MDUU 8.2

Prerequisites for Banner INTTRACKUK 9.3.28.1

The minimum required releases for Banner INTTRACKUK 9.3.28.1 are as follows:
  • Banner INTTRACKUK 8.3.8
  • Banner INTTRACKUK 9.3.25
