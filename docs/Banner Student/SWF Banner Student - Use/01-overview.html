Overview

The Standard Workload Form (SWF) applies to the Ontario colleges and is regulated by the CAAT Collective Agreement. The SWF software solution facilitates the business needs for both college administrators and faculty members.

SWFs are issued to full-time teaching faculty members only. Each faculty member and the college administrators are required to sign off SWFs.

The faculty members’ workload per week is generated in the SWFs based on the following items:
  • Teaching contact hours
  • Attributed hours for preparation
  • Attributed hours for evaluation and feedback
  • Attributed hours for complementary functions

Usually, during an academic term there will be one SWF for each faculty member. However, in some cases, multiple SWFs are necessary. SWF data is reported to the Collective Bargaining Information Services (CBIS) of the Ministry of Labour by 30th of June each year.

Banner SWF provides your institution with the capability of the following main features.
  • Importing third-party data (faculty member and course information) with Banner Mass Data Update Utility (MDUU).
  • Generating SWFs of a new term from three data sources.
  • Performing enrollment audit on SWFs.
  • Creating multiple what if scenarios by the administrative users during the SWF creation phase.
  • Generating faculty members’ SWFs with automatic assignments of preparation types and overtime.
  • Changing SWF status to lock SWF records or to complete the SWF procedure with a MDUU process.
  • On-line viewing and printing SWFs and SWF timetables of faculty members by SWF administrators, school deans, and associate deans.
  • Faculty members’ on-line Self Service for viewing, printing, commenting, and signing their SWFs.
