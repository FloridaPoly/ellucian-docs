Release 9.3.32.2

This release note describes new features, enhancements, and updates in Banner ARSSDC.

The changes are addressed in PR00042907 in the Ellucian Customer Center.

Banner 8 Self-Service is in sustaining support

As of October 1, 2025, Banner 8 Self-Service has moved out of Maintenance Support and entered Sustaining Support through December 31, 2025.

During this time, Ellucian will provide support only for critical vulnerabilities.

Sustaining support does NOT include:
  • Regulatory updates
  • Other security issues not classified as critical vulnerabilities
  • Functional defect fixes

This sustaining support period is intended only to help institutions complete their transition off Banner 8 Self-Service. On January 1, 2026, Banner 8 Self-Service will reach End of Support.

If your institution has not yet transitioned, Ellucian strongly urges you to finalize your plans now. Your Customer Success Manager can connect you with resources and support to ensure a successful move.

For detailed information on support statuses, please review our Support Assurance Policy.

Supporting documentation

To download supporting documentation for this release, click Attachment on this page and then select the associated compressed file (.zip) from the displayed list. The zip file contains the Banner ARSSDC Sales Documents - Resolution Report 9.3.32.2 file.

Release 9.3.31 and 8.8

These release notes describe new features, enhancements, and updates in Banner ARSSDC.

These changes are addressed in PR00041153 & PR00041154 in the Ellucian Customer Center.

Banner 8 Self-Service sustaining support

To support institutions in the final stages of their transition off Banner 8 Self-Service, Ellucian will amend the Support Assurance Policy to provide limited support for Banner 8 Self-Service during the sustaining support phase, October 1 through December 31, 2025. During this time, support will be limited to addressing critical vulnerabilities only. This limited extension is intended to help institutions finalize their transition off Banner 8 Self-Service.

This additional support does NOT include:
  • Regulatory updates
  • Other security issues not classified as critical vulnerabilities
  • Functional defect fixes

If you have not yet transitioned off Banner 8 Self-Service, Ellucian strongly encourages you to contact your Customer Success Manager to finalize your plans, as help and resources are available.

For detailed information on support statuses, please review our Support Assurance Policy.
