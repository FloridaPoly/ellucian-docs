Release 9.3.40.1 and 8.37.1

The Banner Student 9.3.40.1 and 8.37.1 release includes quality improvements and technical currency updates.

Support for Banner 8 Self-Service has ended

As of January 1, 2026, Banner 8 Self-Service has moved out of Sustaining Support and has entered End of Support.

For detailed information on support statuses, please review our Support Assurance Policy.

New admissions-application-contacts-cohorts-attributes BPAPI

The new admissions-application-contacts-cohorts-attributes Banner BPAPI (v1.0.0) for the SAAADMS administrative page deletes only the student admissions attributes from the SGRSATT table.

The Delete request method is supported.

This enhancement addresses PE0007545 in the Ellucian Customer Center.

New table for term based hold data

A new Banner Student table SPRTHLD is introduced to store registration holds by academic term. This enhancement centralizes term-based hold data, ensuring consistent management and providing the foundation for future term-based hold functionality.

This enhancement addresses PE0007639 in the Ellucian Customer Center.

Enhanced additional-student-information-attribute BPAPI

The additional-student-information-attribute BPAPI (v1.0.0) is enhanced to delete or update the records in the SGRSATT table to support the Early Module Registration.

This enhancement addresses PE0006573 in the Ellucian Customer Center.

ASCGEN enhancements

The following ASCGEN enhancements are available for the Banner Student 9.3.40.1 and 8.37.1 releases.
  • Changed table: The SKRECAF table includes an enhancement where the length of the SKRECAF_OS_HELP_AMOUNT column is increased from NUMBER (4) to NUMBER (5). This enhancement satisfies PE0007574.
  • Changed page: The SORSTLE section of the SOAHELP page includes a new student-entitlement-limit BP-API. This enhancement satisfies PE0007582.
