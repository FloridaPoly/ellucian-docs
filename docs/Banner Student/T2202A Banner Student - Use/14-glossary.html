Glossary

An alphabetical list of technical and functional terms used in Banner T2202A module.

ATP

Actual Term Percentage, the percentage of a CRN that falls within a particular tax year.

Commuters

Canadian students who reside in Canada throughout the tax year while attending an institution in the United States.

CRA

Canada Revenue Agency

Education amount

The number of full-time and part-time months qualifying for a tax credit.

HWE

Hours per Week Equivalent per CRN, used in the calculation of full-time and part-time qualifying months. Determines number of hours of enrollment as date-based rather than term-based.

Relevé 8

The Province of Quebec report that summarizes information required for the student’s parents or sponsor to obtain tax credits for that province. This report only encompasses amounts for full-time attendance set by the province each year. For the year 2000, the amounts allowed are $1650.00 per full-time session, to a maximum of $3300.00 for two full-time sessions.

Residents

Canadian students residing in the United States at any time during the tax year while attending a United States institution.

T2202

The Canada Customs and Revenue Agency report that summarizes information required for Canadian students attending Canadian institutions to obtain education tax credits and tuition tax credits.

This report includes a student's date of attendance, number of months of full-time and part-time enrollment at the institution in a qualifying educational program (education amount), and all tuition fees for full-time and part-time studies paid in a calendar year (tuition tax credits).

TFC

Tuition Fees Certificate.

TL11A

The TFC for Canadian students attending United States institutions who are designated as residents.

TL11C

The TFC for Canadian students attending United States institutions who are designated as commuters.

TTC

Tuition Tax Credit, eligible tuition and fees that can be claimed as a tax credit in Canada. The TTC is reported on a TFC.

VTP

Valuated Term Percentage, the sum of the result of multiplying the ATP for a CRN by its HWE in a particular term. Used in the WTP calculation. VTP=(sum of ATP*HWE)

For example, if C1 ATP=.45 HWE=3, C2 ATP=.33 HWE=4, then VTP=(.45*3)+(.33*4)=2.67.

WTP

Weighted Term Percentage, the weighted average percentage of all ATPs applied for all CRNs for a particular term. WTP=VTP/(HWE sum).

For example, if C1 HWE=3, C2 HWE=4, VTP=2.67, then WTP=2.67/3+4)=.38 or 38%.
