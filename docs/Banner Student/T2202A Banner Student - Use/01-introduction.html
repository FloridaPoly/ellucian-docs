Introduction

The Banner T2202A Handbook provides information on the procedures, processes, and so on, required to produce T2202, Relevé 8 (RL-8), and TL11x forms.

T2202 and Relevé 8 forms

Canadian institutions produce T2202 forms (or Relevé 8 forms for Quebec students) for students attending the institution. The forms are a tuition tax receipt and are generated by institutions for students to use in determining their income tax.

TL11A and TL11C forms

The Canadian government provides tax relief for Canadian students studying at post-secondary institutions in the United States. United States institutions can process TL11As and TL11Cs for Canadian students.
