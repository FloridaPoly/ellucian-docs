Processes and Reports (US Institutions)

This chapter describes the following processes and reports.

TL11x Calculation Q.M.-TTC Process (SVPTAXU)

This process calculates the qualifying months and the eligible Tuition Tax Credits (TTCs) for all selected students in the tax year. There are two distinct groups of students that need to be processed by United States institutions: residents and commuters.

This process saves errors, notifications and warnings to the SVRTMSG table when the process is run in Update mode. The messages can be viewed from the SVITMSG page.

If the student program has a value, then the process will insert a value into the Program field (SVBTAXC_PROGRAM) on the SVATAXU page, and the Degree field (SVBTAXC_DEGREE_CODE) will have a null value. If the student program does not have a value, then the process will insert a value into the Degree field (SVBTAXC_DEGREE_CODE), and the Program field (SVBTAXC_PROGRAM) will have a null value.

The CRN dates are used to calculate qualifying months, and to prorate the TTC even when the CRN is outside or totally within the tax year and the WTP is zero. This allows users to identify terms that have sections in the tax year even though the term's start and end dates are not within that tax year.

If the student has a record in the tax year already and the SVBTAXC_EXCEPTION_LOCK is set to Y, the process will not continue for that student. The process can be run for individual students or by population selection. A parameter exists that determines if the process is for residents (R) or commuters (C). The default is R. In the case where a record exists in the same tax year (where the tax year is not being crossed) and the RESI_CDE is different from the one in the record, the new RESI_CDE will be assumed to be correct and the student's record will be updated accordingly. Each institution can build their own population selection, and determine if a student is a resident or commuter.

Note: Most institutions will only need one population selection for resident because there are few commuter students.

This process calculates ancillary amounts referencing detail codes that are associated with the current year on SVAANCI and populates the results in the SVBTAXT table. If the current tax year is 2014, and if a split term involved in a calculation started in 2013, the ancillary amount is prorated using the WTP. If the current tax year is 2015 or later, and if the term involved in a calculation that started in 2014 or later, for commuter students, the ancillary amount is the remaining amount from previous years, and for resident students, the ancillary amount is the minimum of the remaining ancillary amount from previous years and the prorated ancillary amount.

If no qualifying months or TTCs are calculated, then no records are inserted into the SVBTAXC, SVBTAXT, SVBTAXS or SVBTAXR tables. When recalculating a student using the SVPTAXU process, and the student already has a record in the SVBTAXC table and the new calculation results do not have months or TTCs, then a new record is created showing zero months and zero TTCs.

The SVATAXU page is used to view the data generated by the SVPTAXU process. This report can be run from the SVATAXU page or the GJAPCTL page.

CRN processing - SVPTAXU

The CRN dates are used to calculate qualifying months, and to prorate the TTC even when the CRN is outside or totally within the tax year and the weighted term percentage (WTP) is zero.

This allows you to identify terms that are partially in the tax year even though the term's start and end dates are not within that tax year.

This process calculates and stores WTP and HWE data using the CRN or Meeting Time start and end dates instead of the term start and end dates.

The SVPTAXU process compares the tax year end date to the maximum end dates for the Term and the CRNs, Meeting Times, or Student Rules. If the tax year end date is less than (ends before) the other dates, the WTP is used to allocate the fees to the term. Basically, the process looks to see how much of the time is within the tax year to calculate the WTP. On the other hand, if the tax year end date is greater than the maximum CRN, Meeting Times, Student Rules and Term, then the Remaining Balance indicator is marked with a Y and gives all of the fees minus any fees that were used in the prior year.

Full-time months calculation - SVPTAXU

This section discusses the full-time calculation duration for a student

For a student who studies at a US institution as a commuter, if the duration of the qualifying education program is more than three consecutive weeks with at least 10 hours per week, then the month in which the courses start or finish can be qualified as a full-time month even though the courses did not take three weeks in those months. This applies to a program with associated courses/terms that spans one or two tax years. For the tax years before 2011, the program is required to lead to a degree; however, for 2011 and subsequent years, the program is not required to lead to a degree.

When the full-time qualifying month calculation is completed for a tax year, if January or December does not qualify as a full-time month yet, the process determines whether a program with associated courses/terms spans two tax years. If the courses/terms span two tax years, the process determines whether the student can get a full-time month in January or December for the tax year looking back for the courses/terms started in the previous years or looking forward for the courses/terms ended in the next years.

A student who attends a university outside Canada can get qualifying full-time months when the following are true:
  • The student is enrolled in a qualifying educational program (see CRA’s Information Sheet RC190 for the definition)
  • For the years before 2011, the course/program lasts 13 consecutive weeks with 10 hours or more per week
  • For 2011 and subsequent years, the course/program lasts at least three consecutive weeks with 10 hours or more per week
  • The program course leads to a degree

Part-time months calculation - SVPTAXU

The SVPTAXU process gives part-time months to a student (Commuter to the United States) if the CRN has twelve hours in a month.

The criteria is that the student does not have to attend the course for a minimum of twelve hours in the month from which they are withdrawing (assuming the student passes the three consecutive week rule) but the course must have at least twelve hours in the month. Therefore, the calculation date and the reporting date can be different.

A student who attends a University outside Canada as resident can get qualifying part-time months for 2011 and subsequent years only. This is when the following conditions are met:
  • The student is enrolled in a specified educational program (see CRA's Information Sheet RC190 for the definition)
  • The program lasts at least three consecutive weeks and the total hours is not less than 12 in a month, and at least one CRN/term lasts three consecutive weeks (or more) with hours greater than zero per week.
  • The program course leads to a degree
Although the student’s registration date for an early exit status may show the date the student actually withdrew from the CRN, the SVBTAXR_END_DATE column will reflect the date the process used to calculate the HWE.

Depending on the circumstances, a specific calculation might be needed to calculate the part-time months. Otherwise, a non-specific calculation is used to calculate the part-time months.

Specific calculation

A specific calculation will only be used when all of the following occur.

  • The Specific Calc when MT for PT m parameter is set to Y.
  • The student met the CRN calculation rules in the SVAHWER page.
  • The student is in one of situations that are described in the case of specific calculation section in this chapter.
  • At least one of the Days of the Week check boxes was selected on the Meeting Time block on the SSASECT page for a CRN in a term that the student has registered.

The Specific Calc when MT for PT m parameter is used to determine if a specific calculation should be used when meeting times are used to calculate if a month is part-time. The process determines the exact weekdays and weekend days that have meeting hours in the SSRMEET table for students who have a combination of registration status rules, CRN calculation rules, and meeting time records (SSRMEET) for the cases when a specific calculation will be used). The logic for the part-time calculation decides how many hours will be given to the month in which the weeks belong. The original Hours per Week Equivalent (HWE) will be multiplied by a factor, which is the number of days when a student is scheduled to attend a class in a week divided by the total number of days marked in the student meeting record (SSRMEET). If at least one day is not marked in the student meeting records, the process will not use the specific calculation. In other words, the process will use the whole week instead.

The Weekend Management Indicator parameter will not be used if the specific calculation is used.

When the value for the Specific Calc when MT for PT m parameter is Y, the process will consider the following combinations of status, units, and number of SSRMEET records. Open entry/open exit courses - SVPTAXU

When calculating qualifying months using open entry/open exit courses, the HWE is distributed evenly over the number of weeks for the course.

Credits for open entry/open exit courses are set up as the number of overall hours for the course, not per week as for regular courses therefore, before calculating the HWE, the hours are converted to a hours per week equivalent.

Formula: HWE = [UNIT_VALUE * ATOF(HWCC_UNIT_FCTR)] / [weeks between SFRSTCR_ADD_DATE and SFRSTCR_RSTS_DATE ]

In the following samples, the CR for the courses is 32 regardless of whether the student’s course is 2 weeks or 33 ½ weeks.

Sample 1

  • total hours = SFRSTCR_CRED_HR * SVBHWCC_UNIT_FACTOR = 32 * 3 = 96 hours
  • days between SFTSTCR_ADD_DATE and SFRSTCR_RSTS_DATE = 235 days
  • weeks between SFTSTCR_ADD_DATE and SFRSTCR_RSTS_DATE = 235 / 7 = 33.57 weeks
  • HWE = (32 * 3) / 33.57 = 2.859

Sample 2

  • total hours = SFRSTCR_CRED_HR * SVBHWCC_UNIT_FACTOR = 32 * 3 = 96 hours
  • days between SFTSTCR_ADD_DATE and SFRSTCR_RSTS_DATE = 14 days
  • weeks between SFTSTCR_ADD_DATE and SFRSTCR_RSTS_DATE = 14 / 7 = 2 weeks
  • HWE = (32 * 3) / 2 = 14.5
    Note: The course in this example would not qualify on its own because it does not meet the three consecutive week rule.
    If a student gets even a partial decimal as a result of this calculation, then, the student would have to work at least one day into the following week. For example:
    • June 1 - 6 would result in 6 days/7 = .8571or 1 week
    • June 1 - 7 would result in 7 days/7 = 1.0 or 1 week
    • June 1 - 8 would result in 8 days/7 = 1.1429 or 2 weeks

Resident students - SVPTAXU

Students who reside in the United States for even part of the tax year are considered resident for the entire tax year.

Resident students get qualifying months and a Tuition Fees Certificate (TFC) only if they are full-time for at least thirteen weeks for the years before 2011, or at least three weeks for 2011 and subsequent years. If the resident student leaves the program before completing the required weeks, the student does not qualify for months or the TFC. Resident students do not receive a TTC if they do not have qualifying months. The "thirteen week" for the years before 2011, or "three week" for 2011 and subsequent years check will be done after the calculation of the weeks is completed for the year. If a resident student does not qualify for the months, the SVPTAXU process will not check to see if any eligible fees were paid for any particular term. In rare cases where certain medical conditions apply, resident students do not have to meet the thirteen or three week requirement. In these instances, the SVATXEU page will be used to manually create the data. Resident students receive a TL11A page.

The SVPTAXU process checks for a minimum of thirteen consecutive weeks for the years before 2011, or three weeks for 2011 and subsequent years and will grant a month for each month or portion of a month within those weeks. The student is awarded the month even if only one day of the month is within the consecutive week pattern.

In the case of a split term, where the TTC was not calculated in the before the tax year because the resident student did not have thirteen weeks for the years before 2011, or three weeks for 2011 and subsequent years, but does have those weeks in the current tax year, then the amount calculated will be the MIN (Remaining, TTC*WTP). The resident student should receive months and fees. In the case where the resident has months but no fees because none of the terms in the tax year had thirteen or more weeks for the years before 2011, or three weeks or more for 2011 and subsequent years, then an ERROR will be displayed.

Fee Calculation using CRN Rules: When CRN rules are used, the SVPTAXU process checks that there are at least thirteen consecutive weeks for the years before 2011, or at least three weeks for 2011 and subsequent years between the start and end date for all CRNS for each term from the SVAFRYR page. Dates attributed to early exit (SFRSTCR_RSTS_DATE) are also included. If there are less than thirteen or three weeks depending on the tax year, no record will be added to the SVBTAXT table for that term. When calculating the fees for split terms, if a student has a residence code that is different than the one used for the previous tax year, then an ERROR should be displayed showing the ambiguity. If that situation exists, the case should be entered on the SVATXEU page for exceptions.

Fee Calculation using Student Rules: When student rules are used, the SVPTAXU process checks that there are at least thirteen consecutive weeks for the years before 2011, or at least three weeks for 2011 and subsequent years between the start and end dates as assigned in the student rules. For example, the summer term is July 3 to August 30 in 2010, but the student rule is built to give the student the month of June as well. Because of the student rule, the student would qualify for the months and receive the fees because there are thirteen weeks between June 1 and August 30. The SVPTAXU process takes into account the early exit options for student rules. When there is an early exit, SVRESTS_ESTS_CODE is the start date of the term, and the SFBETRM_ESTS_DATE is the end date. If there are less than thirteen weeks, no record will be added to the SVBTAXT table for that term. There is an additional check to avoid giving months to a student for a month that begins or ends on an overlapping week where the student was not actually registered in a course for the month in question. When calculating the fees for split terms, if a student has a Residence code that is different than the one used for the previous tax year, then an ERROR should be displayed showing the ambiguity. If that situation exists, the case should be entered on the SVATXEU page for exceptions.

Commuter students - SVPTAXU

Commuter students are processed the same way as Canadian students attending institutions in Canada.

Both full-time and part-time months apply, and the student can qualify for a TTC even if the student does not qualify for qualifying months, and only three consecutive weeks are required. There is no provision for charitable donations for students commuting to a United States school.

Open Learning Registration - SVPTAXU

The SVPTAXU process processes CRNs created through the Open Learning Registration (OLR) enhancement. This process handles OLR the same way that the T2202A Calculation Q.M.-TTC Process (SVPTAXC).

Eligible application fees paid through Banner Self-Service - SVPTAXU

This process captures TL11 eligible application fees paid through Banner Self-Service. Individuals can make credit card payment through Self-Service. If the individual does not have a Banner PIDM when the payment is made, the transactions are stored in the TBBMISC or TBRMISD table.

The SVPTAXU process checks every student to see if any eligible application fees were paid through Self-Service. If yes, the amount is added to the TL11 tax credit total amount. This amount is calculated regardless of the method of calculation (payments or charges). The Process Appl Fees Paid BSS parameter indicates if these amounts will be calculated. Some users have the application amounts forced into the TBRACCD table and will not want to count it twice.

Only amounts attached to valid TL11 tax type codes will be processed (that is, the value for the TTVTAXT_TAXT_IND field should be Y for the tax type code that is being processed).

Only amounts attached to valid TL11 terms for the tax year will be processed, which means the that the term must exist on the SVRFRYR table.

It is not necessary that the term in which students paid the application fees is the same term in which the students enroll; however, the terms must be in the same tax year.

Program/degree/campus codes selection - SVPTAXU

The program/degree and campus codes calculation or selection is based on SGBSTDN records and SFBETRM and TBRACCD tables.

For each term in SFBETRM and TBRACCD, the process uses the record from SGBSTDN with the matching term; if there is no matching term, the process uses the SGBSTDN record for the term with the most recent start date prior the start date of the term processed (in SFBETRM and TBRACCD).

The process checks if there is registration information for the student in the SFRSTCR table for the SGBSTDN terms when more than one term share the same start date. If only one term has registration information, then the one will be selected. If multiple terms or no terms have registration information, then the selection will be based on the Mode Same Start Date Term field on SVATAXL.

If there is more than one term in SGBSTDN with the same start date, the Mode Same Start Date Term field on SVATAXL is used to determine which term is to be used when two or more term codes share the same start date in the Student Table (SGBSTDN): the maximum term code (1) or the term based on the student's registration and the priority of the term (2).

For each term in SFBETRM and TBRACCD, the campus specified in the SGBSTDN record returned by the process is used to populate the Tuition Tax Credit Eligible Fees block of SVATAXU.

When the last term is processed from SFBETRM and TBRACCD, the program/degree from the corresponding SGBSTDN record will be populated in SVBTAXC record for the student.

Time status - SVPTAXU

The SVPTAXU page allows you to set the time status.

When running the process, you can indicate whether the system is to use the latest time status as specified on the SFBETRM table or the historical effective time status as specified on the SFRTHST table to calculate the qualifying months for which a student would be eligible in a tax year.

If you specify 1 for the Time Status Mode parameter, the process uses the time status from SFBETRM to determine if it, along with another factors such as program, campus, and so on., matches a student calculation rule or exclusion rule and then calculates the hours per week for the entire period of the term defined in the matched calculation rule.

If you specify 2 for the Time Status Mode parameter, the process first determines whether the student has multiple time status records on SFRTHST for the specified term. If so, the process uses the historical effective time status method; otherwise, the process uses the latest time status method as if you had specified 1 for the Time Status Mode parameter.

The rest of this section provides detailed explanation and examples of the historical effective time status method.

The SVPTAXU process treats every time status of a student as a sub-term that needs to be calculated separately. The process checks all time status records for which effective dates are before the end date of the term (as defined on STVTERM), along with another factors such as program, campus, and so on, to determine whether a student exclusion or calculation rule (based on settings on SVAHWER) is matched, using the following sequence.

  1. If a student exclusion rule is matched, the time status record is not considered as a contribution of hours per week in its sub-term for the qualifying month calculations.
    Note: If a user configures the same rule as both a student calculation rule (inclusion) and a student exclusion rule, which is not recommended, then if a time status record matches the student exclusion rule, the process will not take the time status record for the calculations (qualifying months) as it keeps track of what was excluded originally.
  2. If a student exclusion rule is not matched, the process determines whether the time status record matches a calculation rule, and if so, the time status record is considered as a contribution of hours of per week in its sub-term for the qualifying month calculations.
  3. If none of the time status records in a term are detected as excluded or included, the process will go through CRN rules in the same term.
  4. If at least one of the time status records in a term is detected as excluded or included, the process will not go through CRN rules in the same term.

When using SFRTHST, take note of the following considerations and restrictions.

  • All of your institution’s time status rules must be defined on SVAHWER.
  • Any time status value that is not included on SVAHWER will not be considered when matching rules for students.
  • The values in the From Date and To Date fields for the student calculation rules defined on SVAHWER are ignored. Instead, the start and end dates of the term (defined on STVTERM) are used to determine which months are included in the term for which a rule is being evaluated.
  • If the time status effective date (SFRTHST_TMST_DATE) is later than the last day of the tax year or the end date of the term, the time status record is not considered, and the calculation does not evaluate it.
  • The Full/Part-time Students field on SVAHWER is used to determine whether a time status value is to be treated as part time (P) or full time (F).
  • The coverage of months is based on the months in which the term’s start date and end date fall.
The time status effective dates are used to divide the entire term into multiple sub-terms and then to calculate the start date and end date of all the sub-terms. The start date of first sub-term and the end date of last sub-term are recalculated as follows:
  • The start date for the first effective time status sub-term that meets a calculation rule is extended or reduced to the first day of the month that the start-of-term date falls in. If there are multiple time status records for which effective dates are before the start-of-term date, the one that is closest to the start-of-term date is used as the first effective time status sub-term, and the others are not considered in the calculations.
  • The end date of the last effective time status sub-term that meets a calculation rule is extended to the last day of the month in which the end-of-term date falls.
The calculated start and end dates of each time status sub-term are populated into the Start Date field (SVBTAXS_START_DATE) and End Date field (SVBTAXS_END_DATE) in the Student Rule Calculation Detail block of the TL11x Taxation (SVATAXU) page. The values in the From Date field (SVBTAXT_FROM_DATE) and To Date field (SVBTAXT_TO_DATE) on the Tuition Tax Credit Eligible Fees block of SVATAXU are the earliest start date and latest end date of all the calculated time status sub-terms in a term. However, if none of the time statuses in a term match a student calculation rule, and if one or more time statuses match student exclusion rules in the same term, the values in the From Date and To Date fields will be the start date and end date of the term.

The process populates the Student Calculation Rule Number field (SVBTAXS_HWSC_RULE_NUM) or the Student Exclusion Rule Number field (SVBTAXS_HWSE_RULE_NUM) of the Student Rule Calculation Detail block of SVATAXU with the inclusion or exclusion rule number from SVAHWER that was detected when a time status record was matched to a student calculation or exclusion rule in a term. This applies for both time status modes: “Use latest time status in SFBETRM” and “Use effective time status in SFRTHST.”

The Hours per Week Equivalent field (SVBTAXS_HWE) on the Student Rule Calculation Detail block of SVATAXU for each time status sub-term is populated with the value in the Full-time Hours per Week or Part-time Hours per Week field (as appropriate) of the Education/Tuition Amount Limits (SVATAXL) page. This depends on the value (F or P) in the Full/Part-time Students field of the calculation rule to which a time status record matched.

TL11x Laser Report (SVRTXLU)

This process prepares flat files to be merged with Word templates.The templates are available in English and French versions.

The TL11x forms are printed on separate documents. Each of these forms must be signed by an institution official. The report will not print records for students who have neither a program nor a degree code in the SVBTAXC table.

The process checks to see if the Hold Flag (SVBTAXC_HOLD_IND) is set to Y on the SVBTAXC table. If the flag is set to Y, then a record will not be printed. A message will be written to the log file indicating that a record was not printed for that student.

The process reports the total claimable amount on the Reporting Periods window of SVATAXU as the total tax credit.

An error message is shown in the log file, if a term code is associated with multiple reporting periods, but these reporting periods do not completely cover the students' active period in the term (the months between SVBTAXT_FROM_DATE and SVBTAXT_END_DATE). The following is a sample error:

Terms 201705,201709 are missed for Period 3. Check SVATXRP settings. No rec printed. PIDM: 76981, ID: T2CGSYS04, NAME: Lori, Smith

If your institution has multiple campuses, the institution address printed on this report comes from the Campus Address Submission (SVAINAD) page; if your institution has only one campus, the institution address comes from the Installation Control (GUAINST) page. For an institution with multiple campuses, the address reported depends on the campus selected from SVBTAXT by the process. The Mode Same Start Date Term field on the Education/Tuition Amount Limits (SVATAXL page is used by the printing processes to select the correct campus from SVBTAXT table. The indicator provides the ability to determine which term to select when two or more term codes share the same start date (as defined on STVTERM). For web pages and printing processes, the campus is chosen depending on the term code with the latest start date in SVBTAXT. The address associated with the campus on SVAINAD or GUAINST is printed.

If the Program field (SVBTAXC_PROGRAM) on the SVATAXU page has a value, then the value from the SMRPRLE_PROGRAM_DESC field will be used as the program description. If the Program field (SVBTAXC_PROGRAM) has a null value, and the value in the Degree field (SVBTAXC_DEGREE_CODE) has a value, then the value from the STVDEGC_DESC field will be used as the degree description.
