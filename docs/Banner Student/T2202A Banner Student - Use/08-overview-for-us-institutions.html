Overview for US Institutions

The Canadian government provides tax relief for Canadian students studying at post secondary institutions in the United States. United States institutions can process TL11As and TL11Cs for Canadian students.

No web based reports are provided for TL11x forms because an approved representative of the United States institution is required to sign the tax forms.

Many of the same pages and tables used for processing T2202s are used for processing TL11As and TL11Cs. These pages include SVAFRYR, SVATAXL, SVAINAD, SVAESTS, SVARSTS, SVAHWER, and the related tables. The SVPTAXE process and t2202a_load_svbtxtp.shl script also remain the same.

The following assumptions are made:
  • The Tax Code Validation (TTVTAXT) page exists to identify the T2202 eligible tax codes used for TL11As and TL11Cs.
  • The Detail Code Control (TSADETC) page exists to assign TL11x tax type codes to all eligible TL11x detail codes.
  • This TL11A/TL11C solution only applies to Baseline Banner Institutions with local modifications that affect this processing will need to develop conversion procedures to use the new process.
  • Qualifying months for students, when calculated by CRN rules, will not exceed the CRN start and end dates.
