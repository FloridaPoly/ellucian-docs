Intelligent Processes licenses and features

Intelligent Processes offers two product licenses: Task and Holds Automation and Automation.

You can choose one of the two licenses that fits your use case:
  • Task and Holds Automation

    Tailored for efficient bulk task assignments to specific populations. For example, users can effortlessly create or remove holds, manage financial terms, validate profile information, and perform various other tasks at scale.

  • Automation

    Automation encompasses all the features of Task and Holds Automation and goes a step further. It provides users with a powerful solution for managing and optimizing complex approval workflows seamlessly.
