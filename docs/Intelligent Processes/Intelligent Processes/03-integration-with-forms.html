Integration with Forms

Intelligent Processes integrates with the Forms application, allowing you to launch a workflow directly from a form.

Forms is a no-code tool for building, managing, and sharing forms. In Forms, you can design easy to complex forms using multiple fields to drag and drop on the form canvas. You can also preview your form while building and retrieving historical information for each form version.

To get started, create your form, publish it, set permissions, and you can link it to a workflow. When building a workflow, designate the form as your trigger, and then select the associated form you want to use. Additionally, you can use all the data gathered from the form in subsequent steps such as approvals, actions, and reference them in simple tasks.

See the Forms documentation for more information.
